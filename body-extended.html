The experience that came with this track

BODY was a project that took off in my friend's bedroom studio. It was a laptop, MIDI keyboard, connected to a TV while we shared excitement about songs we liked / took inspiration from.

this track was originally a live demo set of the 2 tracks and i decided that Body - Extended should be part of a new era.
