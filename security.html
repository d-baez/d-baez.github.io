where is our 'big data' going

Data that is consistently being collected and stored is often used to enhance the UX, but to what scope. What profiles have companies built of the data they have collected from you.

In my network security class we discuss the CIA triage mean in security terms.

  • C

    Confidentiality

    How Confidentiality is , how secure your sensitive data is protects. How many UIDs have programs, services, clients collected about you?

  • I

    Integrity

    How integrity, is how accessible or trustworthy a service or request to read/see/collect your data. is your information a quick search or cmd+f away from being sought out.

  • A

    Availability

    How Availability, is which users/agents have access to what systems you are currently using.

things you can do to be proactive about your personal data

Accounts

  • Use strong, unique passwords for each of your accounts and consider using a password manager.
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever possible for an added layer of security.
  • Be aware of phishing scams and avoid clicking on suspicious links or attachments.

Network & devices

  • Use a VPN to encrypt your internet traffic and hide your IP address.
  • Regularly update your software and devices to patch security vulnerabilities.
  • Regularly back up your important data to protect against data loss or ransomware attacks.

Privacy

  • Be cautious about the information you share online and on social media platforms.
  • Review and manage your privacy settings on websites and apps to limit data collection.
  • Consider using ad blockers and anti-tracking tools to reduce online tracking.
  • Educate yourself about online security best practices and stay informed about the latest threats and trends in cybersecurity.
