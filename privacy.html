Introduction

Welcome to danielbaez.xyz. I value your privacy and strive to protect your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, how it is used, and how it is safeguarded when you visit this website.

Information We Collect

This site collects a limited amount of information:

How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used for the following purposes:

Cookies

This website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small data files stored on your device that help me understand how you use the site and improve its functionality. Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings; the site remains usable if you do.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to keep your information safe. Despite these efforts, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

These third parties have access to your information only to perform their function and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.
